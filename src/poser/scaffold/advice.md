## Composing here

Reuse intent and preferences in `AGENTS.md`; ask only for consequential missing choices,
accepting delegated choices with stated provisional defaults. Keep the demo and owner's work.
Choose sounds by track role. Plan a composition against the brief, then write a new song,
render and analyze it. Read the report, spectrograms, and history; revise against actual results.
The `humanize` helper can add seeded velocity and timing variation to a line.
Invite the person to audition and give feedback; lack of audition need not block a technically
verified draft when the limitation is disclosed.

## Judging a revision

Compare a revision against an earlier record, and treat a band or bar difference under 1 dB as
possible render noise. Compare relative band levels between nodes and versions, not against
an absolute target. Keep the mix peak at or below -1 dBFS by lowering track `gain`.
For `upstream_failed`, fix the named upstream and render again; for `stale_stem`, render again.
